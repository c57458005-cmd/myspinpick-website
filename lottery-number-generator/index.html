How to Generate Lottery Numbers

Choose a game format such as EuroMillions, Powerball, Mega Millions or UK Lotto, then click Generate Numbers. The tool draws the correct amount of unique numbers from the right ranges for that game — main numbers first, followed by any bonus or star balls. Switch to Custom to set your own pool sizes for raffles, classroom draws or any random pick.

Supported Lottery Formats — EuroMillions, Powerball, Mega Millions & UK Lotto

  • EuroMillions — 5 numbers from 1-50 plus 2 Lucky Stars from 1-12.
  • Powerball — 5 numbers from 1-69 plus 1 Powerball from 1-26.
  • Mega Millions — 5 numbers from 1-70 plus 1 Mega Ball from 1-25.
  • UK Lotto — 6 numbers from 1-59.
  • Custom — set your own main and bonus pool sizes for any random draw.

How the Numbers Are Picked

Every draw works the same way underneath. The tool builds the full pool for the game you selected, shuffles that pool, then takes the first numbers from the shuffled order and sorts them into ascending order.

Because each number leaves the pool as it is taken, a single line can never repeat a number. You will not see the same ball twice inside one set, which is what a real ticket looks like.

Quick Pick or Choosing Your Own Numbers

The generator behaves like a quick pick at a shop till: it chooses for you, instantly, with no favourites and no pattern. That is the honest approach when you have no strong preference.

Many people still keep a personal set — birthdays, anniversaries, a lucky sequence. Both approaches carry exactly the same chance of matching the draw, so the choice is about enjoyment rather than strategy.

Custom Ranges for Raffles and Prize Draws

Switch to Custom and the ranges become yours. Set how many main numbers you want, how high the pool rises, and add bonus balls if the draw needs them.

  • A raffle with 100 tickets — one number from 1 to 100
  • A club draw — three numbers from 1 to 40
  • A classroom prize — one winner from a small numbered list

Reading Your Line and Choosing a Game

Every result is presented the way a ticket reads. Main numbers come first in ascending order, then a plus sign, then any bonus or star balls for the game you picked.

The labels explain the shape of each format: EuroMillions takes five main numbers up to fifty plus two Lucky Stars, Powerball takes five up to sixty-nine plus one Powerball, Mega Millions takes five up to seventy plus one Mega Ball, and UK Lotto takes six numbers up to fifty-nine.

Switch games with a single click and the pool sizes change with the format, so you never have to keep the rules in your head.
