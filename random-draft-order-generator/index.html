Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate a random draft order?

Type teams or players separated by commas, click Generate, and the list is shuffled into a fair random draft order with pick numbers.

Is the draft order really random?

Yes. It uses a Fisher-Yates shuffle in your browser, so every order is equally likely.
