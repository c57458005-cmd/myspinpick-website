How to Use the Wheel Spinner with Random Name Wheel & Custom Lists

Type your names or choices into the box (comma separated), then click "Spin". The wheel spins and lands on a random entry. Save your frequently used lists for quick reuse, and use Remove to delete the last option in the box.

This wheel spinner is built for drawing names from any list — students, teams, prizes and more. Need a different kind of spin? The Related Tools below link to dedicated wheels for random colors, fate-style decisions, dinner picks and quick yes/no calls.

Make Your Own Wheel Spinner (Custom & Random Spinner)

This is a custom wheel spinner — type your own entries instead of using a fixed list. Paste names, numbers, foods or tasks, click Save to keep the list in your browser, and spin again whenever you need a random spinner decision. Nothing is uploaded to a server, so your lists stay private.

Learning how to make your own wheel spinner takes about ten seconds. There is no account to create, no spreadsheet to import and no app to install — the wheel is built from whatever you paste into the box.

  1. Type or paste your entries. Separate them with commas or start each one on a new line. Names, numbers, foods, chores, prizes or trivia answers all work the same way.
  2. Watch the wheel build itself. Every entry gets its own slice and color, so a long list stays readable. Aim for at least four entries — two or three produce a very thin slice that is hard to read.
  3. Spin and remove winners. Click Spin to land on one entry at random. Turn on Remove winner if you want a new result each time instead of repeats.
  4. Save your wheel for next time. Click Save and the list is stored in your browser, ready to reload the next time you visit — this is what makes it a reusable make your own wheel tool rather than a one-off spin.

Because everything runs locally, you can build a wheel with student names, customer entries or prize lists without uploading anything. If you would rather skip the setup, the pre-filled tools below cover the most common cases — pick a color, decide what to eat or get a straight yes or no.
