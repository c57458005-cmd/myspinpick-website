Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

MySpinPick ("we", "our", or "us") operates myspinpick.com. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

MySpinPick tools run entirely in your browser. We do not require accounts, and we do not collect or store the names, items, or numbers you enter into any tool. Your inputs are processed locally on your device and are never sent to our servers.

We collect basic, automatic usage data through Google Analytics, described below.

2. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd.) to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are viewed and how long visits last. Google Analytics collects data such as device type, browser, approximate location (country/city), and pages visited. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

You can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with the official browser add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

MySpinPick itself does not set advertising cookies. Google Analytics may set cookies to distinguish visitors and measure usage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will continue to work.

Some tools also use your browser's local storage to remember what you were doing — for example the lists you save on a wheel or name picker, and your sound on/off setting. This data is stored only on your own device, is never sent to our servers, and is not used for advertising or tracking. To remove it, clear site data for myspinpick.com in your browser settings.

4. Advertising

At this time, MySpinPick does not display third-party advertising. If we introduce advertising in the future, this policy will be updated to describe the advertising partners, the cookies they may use, and your choices, including how to manage personalized ads via Google Ad Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

MySpinPick is a general-audience tool site and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data Security & Retention

Because tool inputs never leave your device, we do not retain them. Analytics data is retained by Google according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
