How to Use the Bubble Level

  1. Open the page on a phone and tap Enable Sensors (iOS may ask for permission).
  2. Lay the phone on a surface and watch the bubble.
  3. When the bubble sits in the center circle, the surface is level.

Desktop browsers usually have no tilt sensor, so the bubble stays centered — use a phone for real readings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the bubble level work?

On a phone, it reads the device's tilt sensors and moves the bubble. When the bubble sits in the center, the surface is level. On a desktop without sensors it shows a note instead.
