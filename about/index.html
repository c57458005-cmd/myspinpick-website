About SpinPick

SpinPick (myspinpick.com) is a collection of free, browser-based random tools: a wheel spinner, name picker, decision wheel, random group and number generators, coin flip, dice roller, lottery numbers, and more.

Why We Built It

Every day brings small decisions: what to eat, who goes first, which task to pick. SpinPick makes those moments quick and fun. Everything runs instantly in your browser — no sign-up, no downloads, no waiting.

Our Principles

  • Free forever. All tools are free to use, with no account required.
  • Privacy-friendly. Your inputs are processed locally in your browser and never sent to our servers. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Fast & simple. Lightweight pages that load quickly on any device, including phones.
  • Fair & random. Results use standard browser randomization — no hidden tricks.

What's Inside

SpinPick currently offers 16 tools across several categories: spin wheels (wheel spinner, decision wheel, what-to-eat wheel, yes/no wheel), random pickers (group generator, number generator, letter picker, draft order), games (coin flip, dice roller, duck race, lottery numbers), and utilities (color picker, metronome, bubble level).
