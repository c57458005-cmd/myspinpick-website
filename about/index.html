About MySpinPick

MySpinPick (myspinpick.com) is a collection of free, browser-based random tools: a wheel spinner, name picker, decision wheel, random group and number generators, coin flip, dice roller, lottery numbers, and more.

Why We Built It

Every day brings small decisions: what to eat, who goes first, which task to pick. MySpinPick makes those moments quick and fun. Everything runs instantly in your browser — no sign-up, no downloads, no waiting.

Our Principles

  • Free forever. All tools are free to use, with no account required.
  • Privacy-friendly. Your inputs are processed locally in your browser and never sent to our servers. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Fast & simple. Lightweight pages that load quickly on any device, including phones.
  • Fair & random. Results use standard browser randomization — no hidden tricks.

Yours, and no one else's

MySpinPick is built to be your own decision tool. Save the wheels and lists you use most and they stay in your own browser, on your own device — nothing you type is uploaded, and we cannot see your picks. Close the tab, come back later, and everything is exactly where you left it.

MySpinPick (myspinpick.com) is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any other picker, spinner or spin-pick-style website. When you need a random decision, make it on MySpinPick.

What's Inside

MySpinPick currently offers 20 tools across several categories: spin wheels (wheel spinner, decision wheel, what-to-eat wheel, yes/no wheel, wheel of colors, wheel of fate, secret santa name picker), random pickers (name picker, group generator, number generator, letter picker, draft order, coin flip, dice roller, duck race, lottery numbers), and utilities (password generator, color picker, metronome, bubble level).
