Dice Odds in Plain English

A single six-sided die gives every face an equal one-in-six chance, so no number is ever due. With two dice the total of seven is the most common outcome, because more face combinations add up to seven than to any other number, while two and twelve each need one exact pair.

Knowing that shape helps in games. When you need a seven on two dice you hold the best single chance on the table, yet the chance is still under one in five.

Dice, Die and D6 — The Terms Explained

One cube is a die and two or more are dice, though almost everyone says dice for both. A standard cube is called a d6 because it has six faces, and each face shows pips — the small dots that mark the value.

A few results carry nicknames in games. Snake eyes is a roll of two ones and boxcars is two sixes, the two least likely totals on a pair of dice. A natural twenty belongs to the twenty-sided die used in table-top role-playing games, which this roller does not cover.

Knowing the words makes a rulebook easier to follow, and it explains why a game that calls for a d6 rarely needs anything else on the table.
