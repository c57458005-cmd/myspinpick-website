About SpinPick

SpinPick is a home for spin-the-wheel and random-pick tools built for real decisions. Spin a wheel of names to choose a student or winner, split a class into fair random groups, decide with a yes/no wheel, draw lottery numbers for a raffle, or flip a coin when you're stuck.

Every tool is free, requires no account, and runs 100% in your browser — your names and results never leave your device.
