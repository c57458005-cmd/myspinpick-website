Free Wheel Spinner & Random Decision Tools

SpinPick is a home for spin-the-wheel and random-pick tools built for real decisions. Spin a wheel of names to choose a student or winner, split a class into fair random groups, decide with a yes/no wheel, or flip a coin when you're stuck. Every tool is free, ad-light and runs 100% in your browser — your names and results never leave your device.

All tools are free to use and require no account or installation. Just open the tool you need, do your work, and copy the result.
