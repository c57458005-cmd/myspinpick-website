About MySpinPick

MySpinPick is a home for spin-the-wheel and random-pick tools built for real decisions. Spin a wheel of names to choose a student or winner, split a class into fair random groups, decide with a yes/no wheel, draw lottery numbers for a raffle, or flip a coin when you're stuck.

Every tool is free, requires no account, and runs 100% in your browser — your names and results never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. All tools process everything locally in your browser. Your text, names and results never leave your device.

Do these tools work on mobile?

Yes. Every tool is responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktops alike.
